THE METHODOLOGY

Every number has a trail.

A conditional token-value scenario built entirely from the sealed snapshot. It estimates neither launch probability nor your potential reward.

Snapshot-relative value

TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity

Peer multiple: median circulating market cap / TVL of token-recorded peers, clipped to 0.1–4.0×. Blend each category median with the global median using n / (n + 6). With zero peers, use the global median.

Growth = 1 + clamp(7d TVL change, −50%, +50%) / 200. Missing growth uses 1.0×.

Maturity = 0.85 + 0.30 × min(1, ln(1 + tracked days) / ln(1096)). Missing listing date uses 1.0×. Tracked age is not proven token-free age.

Low / high scenarios = 0.5× / 1.8× the base value. These are subjective sensitivity bands, not statistical confidence intervals or FDV forecasts.

What the data cannot tell us

Token records may be incomplete. The official 40-name watchlist is authoritative for coverage, not proof of a future launch. Other candidates pass transparent category/name exclusions; parentProtocol is absent, so research candidates may still be duplicates.

TVL is not revenue and can count the same assets across protocols. Circulating-cap comparables do not estimate token supply or FDV. Maturity and growth adjustments are chosen assumptions.

Plans are category-based, illustrative workflows. Fees, minimums, access, supported assets and incentives need live verification. Do not infer a points program from this page.
